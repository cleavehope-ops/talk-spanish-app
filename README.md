# Talk Trip – learn a language by talking

A free, simple web app for practicing everyday conversation. Works in the phone browser, no sign-up, no keys.

- **Trip map:** short spoken scenes (meeting someone, ordering at a café, asking for directions).
- **Speak or tap:** hear each line (normal or slow), see the English and a sound-it-out spelling, answer out loud or tap a reply.
- **Practice cards:** phrases come back with simple spaced review (saved only on your device).
- **Translate chat (demo):** two people, each in their own language. Uses the browser's built-in translator, or the free MyMemory service as a backup (text is sent to MyMemory).

Speech recognition depends on the browser (best in Chrome; Safari on iOS 14.5+). Hand-made lessons: Spanish, French, Haitian Creole, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, German, Mandarin Chinese (with pinyin) and Japanese (with romaji). Any other language can be auto-translated on the fly (may have small mistakes). New languages can be added as JSON files in `packs/`, or loaded in the app with “Add a language file”.
